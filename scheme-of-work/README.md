# GCSE Computer Science Scheme of Work
## Edexcel 1CP2 - 3 Year Delivery Model

This directory contains a complete Scheme of Work for delivering the Edexcel GCSE Computer Science (1CP2) specification over 3 years with 3 hours per week.

## Overview

- **Delivery Model**: 3 years × 33 weeks × 3 lessons per week = ~297 lessons
- **Based on**: Craig'n'Dave resources and teaching methodology
- **Target**: Year 9 start (Key Stage 4 begins Year 10)

## Year Structure

| Year | Focus | SLR Topics |
|------|-------|------------|
| Year 9 | Foundation - Programming & Systems | SLR 1, 2 + Programming fundamentals |
| Year 10 | Core Theory | SLR 3, 4, 5, 6 + Programming consolidation |
| Year 11 | Algorithms & Exam Prep | SLR 9, 10, 11 + Intensive revision |

## Terminology

- **Week**: Calendar week within the academic year (1-33)
- **Lesson**: Individual 1-hour teaching session (3 per week)
- **Term**: ~11 weeks of teaching (3 terms per year)
- **SLR**: Specification Learning Resource (topic numbering from Craig'n'Dave)

## Resources Required

1. Craig'n'Dave YouTube videos (free)
2. Craig'n'Dave T.I.M.E. Workbook (purchasable)
3. Craig'n'Dave Programming Workbook (purchasable)
4. Smart Revise subscription (purchasable)
5. Python IDE (IDLE, VS Code, or Repl.it)

## Customisation

This scheme is designed to be adapted to your school context:
- Adjust week numbers to match your academic calendar
- Modify assessment timing as needed
- Add school-specific resources and activities
- Adapt homework tasks to your homework policy
