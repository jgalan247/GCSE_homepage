# Year 11 Scheme of Work - Algorithms & Exam Preparation
## GCSE Computer Science (Edexcel 1CP2) - 3 Year Delivery Model

**Hours per week:** 3 (3 × 1-hour lessons)
**Weeks per year:** 33 (reduced for exam leave)
**Total lessons:** ~99 (varies based on exam dates)

---
