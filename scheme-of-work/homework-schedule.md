# Homework Schedule
## GCSE Computer Science (Edexcel 1CP2) - 3 Year Delivery Model

This document provides weekly homework tasks aligned with the Scheme of Work.

---

## Homework Policy

- **Frequency**: One homework task per week
- **Time**: 30-45 minutes expected completion time
- **Types**: Video watch + notes, Programming practice, Workbook sections, Smart Revise quizzes
- **Submission**: As per school policy
